### 2. Tyana Davis
#### Product vision statement
 
    Product Name
    Student Planner

    Product Description
    Student Planner is a user-friendly software application designed to help college students organize and manage their academic responsibilities in one convenient place. The application allows students to keep track of assignments, upcoming deadlines, and important tasks throughout the semester.

    The goal of the product is to make it easier for students to stay organized and manage their time. Instead of keeping track of assignments and deadlines in different places, students can use Student Planner to keep their academic tasks organized in one application.

   What the Product Does
       Student Planner will allow users to:

       Add and manage assignments and tasks.
     
       Keep track of assignment due dates.

       View upcoming deadlines.

       Organize academic responsibilities.

       Mark tasks as completed.
       
       Easily see what work needs to be completed.
       
       Manage their schoolwork in one convenient location.

   Intended Users

       The primary users of Student Planner are college students who need help organizing their assignments, deadlines, and academic responsibilities.

    Product Vision statement

    My vision is to create a simple, reliable, and user-friendly student planner that helps college students organize their assignments, deadlines, and tasks in one convenient place. The product will make it easier for students to manage their time, keep track of important academic responsibilities, and stay organized throughout the semester. The application will focus on simplicity, accessibility, and usability while providing features that can be expanded and improved over time.
